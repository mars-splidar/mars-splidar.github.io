MaRS

Markov-Renewal Simulator for High-Flux Single-Photon LiDAR
Weijian Zhang, Author 2, Author 3

MaRS is a fast physics-based simulator for single-photon LiDAR under detector dead-time effects. It enables efficient prediction of photon count statistics and distorted temporal histograms without expensive sequential per-photon simulation.
